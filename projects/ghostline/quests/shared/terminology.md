# Ghostline Terminology

## Ghostline

The broker collective employing V across the series.

## Quiet Spine

The canonical name of the identity-laundering route first exposed by the
`gq001` cache.

## Retained Route Names

The following names remain available for future routes or aliases:

- Dead Channel
- Black Lantern
- Kintsugi Line
- Paper Tiger Route

## Black Lantern

A paired human-courier operation inside Quiet Spine. One courier carries
mnemonic fragments and the other carries the cipher needed to identify and
reconstruct them.
