# Act IV Script

## Act IV Text

### Patch confession

**Patch:** Iris told you.

**V choice:** You knew about the couriers.

**Patch:** Knew they used people. Did not know they used the people. I was told
the implants carried encrypted noise, useless without a paired key.

**V:** You believed that?

**Patch:** I believed not asking kept the route cheap and me alive. Dress it up
however you want. Still the truth.

**Patch:** I have a van at the safe site. Registry will clear the interchange's
first scan. After that, we improvise in person.

### Vehicle ambient dialogue

These lines can ship as phone-style or HUD text before a dedicated drive scene
exists.

**Patch, after entering:** Van belongs to a plumbing cooperative that has
never fixed a pipe. Try not to add traffic violations to its business model.

**V:** How many courier runs did you touch?

**Patch:** Four sealed cases. Two dead drops. No names.

**V:** Mara had one.

**Patch:** Yeah.

**Patch, mid-drive:** Quiet Spine paid for ignorance by the kilometre. Thought
that made me outside the thing.

**V:** It made you useful to it.

**Patch:** Starting to notice.

**Patch, interchange arrival:** Zero-Seven-B is the refrigerated box truck.
Route badge under the left mirror. Driver should be inside arguing about the
alarm you definitely did not trigger.

**Patch, clean-yard variant:** Driver thinks the schedule changed. We still
have a quiet window.

**Patch, detected-yard variant:** Driver knows the route is burned. Window's
closed; we're going through it anyway.

**Patch, after carrier theft:** Beacon is moving. Relay is south. I'll wipe the
van and find another way there.
