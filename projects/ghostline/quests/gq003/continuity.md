# Black Lantern Continuity

## Continuity Consequences

### Preserve the route

- Set `gq003_choice_preserve = 1` and `gq003_route_preserved = 1`.
- Mara's indexed foreign fragments are scrubbed, but the wider courier index
  and route authentication survive.
- Morrow gains an active Black Lantern trace toward Morita's next handoff.
- Iris cooperates but considers V responsible for any reuse of the identities.
- A later quest may begin from the trace without rebuilding Morita's physical
  trail.

### Burn the identities

- Set `gq003_choice_burn = 1` and `gq003_route_burned = 1`.
- The identity index is destroyed and wipe commands are issued to surviving
  courier packages.
- Morrow loses the clean route to Morita.
- Iris and Cinder treat the act as completing the protection begun at the
  Kabuki relay.
- A later quest must reach Morita through Patch's contacts, a surviving paper
  ledger, or another physical witness.

Both routes keep Morita alive, keep Patch in Ghostline, and preserve enough
story pressure for the next quest. Neither route should be framed as the hidden
correct answer.
