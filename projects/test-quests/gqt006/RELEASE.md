# Cyberpsycho Sighting: Goth Baddie

> Release-page draft. Text under **Pre-release notes** is internal and should be
> removed before publishing.

## Short description

A new adult Cyberpsycho encounter in Kabuki with voiced dialogue, player
choices, a custom opponent, multiple combat outcomes, and an explicit optional
aftermath.

## Description

Regina has another Cyberpsycho Sighting for V. An armed and heavily chromed
woman calling herself Goth Baddie has already put down the NCPD
first-response team in Kabuki—but when V arrives, she offers a different kind
of challenge.

How the encounter ends depends on what you say and who wins the fight. Walk
away, take her deal, defeat her, or lose to her and discover exactly what she
meant by making V hers.

`Cyberpsycho Sighting: Goth Baddie` is a standalone, fully voiced encounter
built to feel like a small piece of the base game. It includes Regina's NCPD
brief, a Cyberpsycho map marker and journal entry, original dialogue and
choices, a custom combat encounter, outcome-sensitive quest flow, a readable
datashard, and bespoke adult scene choreography assembled from Cyberpunk
2077's animation library.

## Features

- A new Cyberpsycho Sighting located in Kabuki.
- A custom female Cyberpsycho with unique appearance, anatomy, equipment, and
  combat tuning.
- Original voiced dialogue for Goth Baddie and both feminine- and
  masculine-voiced V.
- Procedural facial animation for Goth Baddie's dialogue.
- Dialogue choices before and after combat.
- Separate victory, defeat, refusal, lethal, and non-lethal paths.
- An optional explicit aftermath determined by player choice and the outcome
  of the fight.
- A Regina/NCPD phone brief, quest journal presentation, Cyberpsycho map icon,
  readable evidence, and outcome report.
- Packaged as a standalone encounter; the Ghostline quest series is not
  required.

## Content warning

This is an adults-only mod containing explicit sexual content, nudity, combat,
strong language, and consensual dominance/submission themes. The explicit
route can be refused through dialogue.

## Requirements

- Cyberpunk 2077 with Phantom Liberty.
- RED4ext.
- ArchiveXL.
- TweakXL.

Use current versions of the framework mods that support your installed game
version.

## Installation

Extract the archive into the Cyberpunk 2077 installation folder so that the
included `archive` and `r6` folders merge with the folders already there.

The final paths should include:

```text
Cyberpunk 2077\archive\pc\mod\Goth_Baddie.archive
Cyberpunk 2077\archive\pc\mod\Goth_Baddie.archive.xl
Cyberpunk 2077\r6\tweaks\Goth_Baddie\
```

## Starting the encounter

After entering Night City and allowing the configured in-game delay to pass,
Regina will send V an NCPD brief and the Cyberpsycho Sighting will appear in
the journal and on the map. Follow the marker to Kabuki.

## Compatibility

- Designed for both feminine- and masculine-voiced V.
- The mod owns its quest, character, localization, and world resources under
  standalone `gqt006`/`Goth_Baddie` paths.
- No base-game files are intentionally replaced.
- Other mods that alter global player bodies, genital meshes, animation rigs,
  Cyberpsycho systems, or the encounter's Kabuki location may affect visual or
  gameplay results.

## Uninstallation

Remove the following files and folder:

```text
Cyberpunk 2077\archive\pc\mod\Goth_Baddie.archive
Cyberpunk 2077\archive\pc\mod\Goth_Baddie.archive.xl
Cyberpunk 2077\r6\tweaks\Goth_Baddie\
```

As with most quest mods, uninstalling during an active playthrough of the
encounter is not recommended. Finish the quest and make a new manual save
first.
