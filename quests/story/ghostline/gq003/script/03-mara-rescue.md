#### Mara's unsent maintenance ticket

```text
TENANT: MARA VENN
BUILDING: H8 SERVICE STACK / UNIT 42
TICKET: AIR HANDLER CYCLING FALSE HUMIDITY

Replaced the intake sensor again. Controller still reports moisture when the
```

The ticket establishes Mara's real apartment and supplies the image Iris later
uses to separate Mara's own memories from implanted apartments.

### Rescue and escort lines

**Mara, restraint opened:** No. Wait. Which apartment is this?

**V:** Not an apartment. Clinic. Name's V. I'm getting you out.

**Mara:** Mara. I think. Keep saying it if I stop answering.

**Iris, after jammer disabled:** I have her signal. It is carrying six identity
headers and one live panic response. The panic is Mara.

**Mara, escort gate one:** Hallway should turn left here.

**V:** It doesn't.

**Mara:** I know. That's the problem.

**Mara, escort gate two:** Smell that? Burned dust. Unit Thirty-Eight had the
same vent motor.

**V:** Your unit?

**Mara:** I don't know anymore.

**Mara, escort gate three:** Outside. Good. Outside only has one sky.

### Defense lines

**Iris:** Sit her down. I can quiet the foreign loops, but the jammer kept them
compressed. When they rebound, she may not know which body is hers.

**Mara:** I know which body hurts.

**Iris:** Hold the perimeter. Ninety seconds.

**Mara, first pressure beat:** Someone is unlocking my front door.

**V:** Nobody's here but us.

**Iris:** Keep her on your voice. Do not argue with the memory; give her
something present.

**Mara, second pressure beat:** Blue wall. Water stain over the kitchen light.

**V:** Asphalt. Rain. Gunfire. Stay with those.

**Iris, stabilization complete:** Foreign loops are isolated. She is still
Mara.

**Mara:** That sounded less certain in your head.

### Second Iris scene

Mara remains physically present at the safe site, seated or leaning against a
work surface. The first implementation may keep her silent during the formal
scene so the production scene remains a two-performer Iris/V conversation.
Her pre-scene and post-scene ambient lines preserve her presence.

#### Opening

**Mara, pre-scene ambient:** This room only belongs to one person. I checked.

**Iris:** Mara's own memories are holding. The foreign ones are indexed, not
gone. Pair Zero-Seven-B can still name every identity inside her.

#### Choice group one

**Optional choice: “Can you remove the memories?”**

**V:** Can you take the other memories out?

**Iris:** I can cut their index and let them decay. Pulling at the memories
themselves would tear through Mara's alongside them. The implant no longer
knows where one life ends and another begins.

**Optional choice: “What happens if we recover the cipher?”**

**V:** We get the cipher. What exactly does it give us?

**Iris:** Names, source fragments, destination identities, every courier who
carried them. Enough to restore stolen lives. Enough to sell them again.

**Progression choice: “Morrow knew?”**

**V:** Morrow knew this was possible?

**Iris:** He suspected. Now he calls the index a route to Morita. He is not
wrong. That is what makes his answer dangerous.

#### Choice group two

**Optional pressure: “Patch sent her into this?”**

**V:** Did Patch put Mara on the route?

**Iris:** He moved sealed implants between two handoffs. He says he never chose
the carriers. Ask whether ignorance was part of the price or part of the
service.

**Progression choice: “Where's the vehicle?”**

**V:** Where is Zero-Seven-B now?

**Iris:** Northside freight interchange. The beacon marked its parking row.
Patch has a vehicle that will pass the first gate.

**Iris:** Morrow sees a route to the person at the top. I see a list of people
the route can hurt again.

#### Close

**V:** Keep Mara stable. I'll bring you the cipher.

**Iris:** Bring it to the relay, not here. If it opens in this room, every
memory in it gets another chance to become someone.

**Mara, post-scene ambient:** Find the apartment with the green kitchen. I
remember the key. I want to know whose hand it belongs to.

Scene exit: `continue_route`.
