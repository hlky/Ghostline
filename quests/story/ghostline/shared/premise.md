# Ghostline Series Premise

Ghostline is a loose netrunner cell operating from half-abandoned telecom
infrastructure in Watson and Japantown. It is a broker collective rather than a
conventional street gang: intrusion specialists, signal thieves, courier
coders, braindance editors, and blackmail archivists. Ghostline hires outside
muscle when a job crosses into meatspace.

The opening arc follows Quiet Spine, an identity-laundering route protected by
Tyger Claws and Arasaka-adjacent interests. What first appears to be covert data
transport becomes a system for moving fragments of real people through
unwitting human couriers.
