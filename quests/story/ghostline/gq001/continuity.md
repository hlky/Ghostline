# Ghostline Continuity

`gq001` replaces the `gq000` prototype as the first canonical series event.
Its outgoing story state is:

- V has worked for Patch and Ghostline.
- V extracted the Quiet Spine cache.
- V met Iris and learned that the cache contains identity fragments and memory
  edits.
- Iris retained enough reconstructed information to identify the later Black
  Lantern route.

Later quests should not import `gq000` as a separate completed job.
